# Full Lecture Summary

IT3120 - Modern Enterprise Management is a **4-credit** module coordinated by Prof. Dasuni Nawinna and delivered across Malabe, Kandy Uni, and Northern delivery locations.

The module has five learning outcomes:

1. Explain organizational functions within IT businesses or units.
2. Apply management concepts in IT organizations.
3. Explain how a new IT business organization is developed.
4. Apply project-management principles and tools in industry.
5. Evaluate legal and ethical aspects of IT businesses and projects.

Weekly delivery consists of:

- A three-hour lecture-related session containing lecture, tutorial, and quiz activities.
- A two-hour workshop containing group presentations assessed by two panel members.

Assessment is divided into:

- In-lecture quizzes: 10%.
- Workshops: 20%.
- Midterm: 20%.
- Final exam: 50%.

The slide states that students must achieve at least 50% from CA and 50% from the final exam.

Workshops require groups of 6-8 students. Scheduled groups present for 10 minutes and answer questions for 5 minutes. Two staff members evaluate the presentations. Quizzes are delivered during weekly lectures through Moodle, are randomized and auto-graded, last 15 minutes, and collectively contribute 10%.

The delivery schedule begins with management concepts and business functions, places the midterm in Week 8, and then covers project-management topics from Weeks 9-12. Week 13 is used to complete evaluations.

Immediate student responsibilities include completing registration, obtaining Courseweb access, finalizing workshop groups in Week 1, and joining the student WhatsApp group using the link supplied through Courseweb.
