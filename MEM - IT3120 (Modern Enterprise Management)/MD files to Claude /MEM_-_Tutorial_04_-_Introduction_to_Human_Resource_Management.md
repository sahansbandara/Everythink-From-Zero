## Exam-Focused Study Guidance

> **Supplementary guidance:** The following section identifies what each question expects you to prepare. It does not claim to reproduce answers from the uploaded PDF.

### Major Topic 1 - HRM Fundamentals (Questions 1-5)

**Questions covered:**
- Q1: Definition of HRM
- Q2: Two key focuses of HRM
- Q3: Strategic function of HRM
- Q4: Three core HR functions
- Q5: Importance of HRM in the IT industry

**Exam Tip:** Prepare one precise definition of **Human Resource Management**, then be able to expand it using the ideas of people, organizational goals, strategic contribution, and major HR activities.

**Common Mistake:** Do not treat HRM as only recruitment. The tutorial explicitly separates recruitment from broader HRM functions, so HRM clearly covers a wider scope.

**Likely question form:**

**Question:** Define HRM and explain why it is considered strategic.  
**Expected answer scope:** Definition + relationship between managing people and achieving organizational/business goals.

**Questions covered:**
- Q6: Recruitment and business goals
- Q7: HR recruitment process
- Q8: Modern recruitment trends in the IT industry

**Process representation:**

```text
Business / workforce need
        |
        v

        |
        v

        |
        v
```

**Exam Tip:** Question 7 asks for an **ordered process**, so revise recruitment as a sequence rather than as disconnected terms. Question 8 expects current IT-industry recruitment practices and examples.

**Common Mistake:** Do not answer Q6 by simply listing recruitment steps. It asks **how recruitment supports business goals**, so the answer must connect hiring decisions to organizational performance.

**Likely question form:**

**Question:** Outline the recruitment process and explain how it supports business goals.  
**Expected answer scope:** Ordered recruitment stages + a clear business-goal connection.

### Major Topic 3 - Performance Management (Questions 9-10)

**Questions covered:**
- Q9: Performance management cycle
- Q10: Two components of performance management

**Concept structure:**

```text
Performance Management
|
+-- Cycle / repeated process
|
+-- Individual components
```

**Exam Tip:** Keep the **cycle** and the **components** separate. A cycle normally requires the stages to be explained in order, while a component question asks you to identify specific elements and explain each one.

**Common Mistake:** Giving only a list when the question uses the word **explain**. Each identified component needs a brief explanation.

**Likely question form:**

**Question:** Explain the performance-management cycle and describe two of its components.  
**Expected answer scope:** Ordered cycle + two clearly explained components.

- Q11: Financial vs non-financial rewards, with examples

**Required comparison structure:**

|---|---|---|
| Main distinction | Prepare the monetary side | Prepare the non-monetary side |
| What the question requires | Explanation + example | Explanation + example |
| Exam technique | Contrast directly | Contrast directly |

**Exam Tip:** Use a side-by-side comparison. The question explicitly asks for the **difference** and **examples**, so both are required.

**Common Mistake:** Giving examples without first explaining the distinction between the two categories.

**Question:** Differentiate financial and non-financial rewards with suitable examples.

### Major Topic 5 - Leadership and Organizational Power (Questions 12-13)

**Questions covered:**
- Q12: Leadership vs management
- Q13: Two types of power with IT examples

**Comparison framework:**

| Topic | What the tutorial expects |
|---|---|
| Leadership vs management | A direct comparison, not two unrelated definitions |
| Types of organizational power | Identify two types, explain each, and apply each to an IT example |

**Exam Tip:** Q13 is application-oriented. Prepare examples involving IT roles such as project teams, technical leads, managers, architects, developers, or specialists.

**Common Mistake:** For Q12, avoid writing only “leadership is X” and “management is Y.” Make the differences explicit.

**Likely question form:**

**Question:** Compare leadership and management, then explain two forms of power using IT-related examples.  
**Expected answer scope:** Structured comparison + two explained power types + relevant IT examples.

```text
          |
```

**Exam Tip:** Q14 asks for **three characteristics**, while Q15 asks for an explanation of **influence**. For Q15, show a cause-and-effect relationship between individual traits and team performance.

**Likely question form:**

**Question:** Describe characteristics of an effective project team and explain how personality can influence its performance.  
**Expected answer scope:** Three team characteristics + explanation of personality-to-team-performance effects.
