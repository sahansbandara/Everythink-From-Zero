## Page 01 — IT3120 Module – Workshop Presentation Instructions

### Original Page Content

There will be a total of **five workshop presentations** for the IT3120 module throughout the semester.

#### Group Evaluation and Attendance

- All presentations will be evaluated on a **group basis**.
- Every group member must be present during the scheduled presentation session.
- If a group member is absent, that individual will **not be awarded any marks** for the presentation.
- Therefore, all groups and all group members are required to attend the workshop.

#### Presentation Duration

- Each group will be allocated **10 minutes** to deliver the presentation.
- The presentation will be followed by a short **question-and-answer session**.

#### Selection of Presenters

- Only **one or two members** from each group are required to deliver the presentation.
- Members who present during a workshop presentation **cannot present again in the upcoming workshop presentations**.
- Every group member must take a turn presenting during the semester.
- The order of presenters must be discussed and decided among the group members in advance.

#### Presentation Schedule

- The schedule for **Presentation 1** is currently available.
- The schedules for the remaining presentations will be updated on a weekly basis.
- Students are responsible for checking the published schedules and preparing accordingly.

#### Peer Reviewing

- Groups that are not presenting during a particular workshop will perform the role of **peer reviewers**.
- Marks will be awarded to students who actively participate in the peer-reviewing process, including asking relevant questions and providing constructive feedback.
- Since both presentation and peer-review participation contribute to the assessment, **all groups must be present at every scheduled workshop session**.

### Explanation

The workshop assessment is not limited to the day a group gives its presentation. Every group must attend every scheduled session because students are assessed through two roles:

- **Presenter role:** One or two selected members deliver the group presentation.
- **Peer-reviewer role:** Members of non-presenting groups ask relevant questions and provide constructive feedback.

The presenter rotation rule ensures that every member develops presentation skills. A member who presents once cannot be selected again for an upcoming workshop presentation until the other members have received their turns. Therefore, the group should create a presenter plan before the presentation series begins.
